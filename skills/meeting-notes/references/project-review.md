# Project Review Template

Give a status only when the source states one or the meeting agreed on it.

```markdown
# Project Review: [project]

**Date:** [date] · **Phase:** [phase] · **Project status:** [On track | At risk | Off track] · **Status:** Draft

## Progress
- **Completed:** [milestones]
- **In progress:** [current work]
- **Next:** [next milestones]

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Actual |
|--------|--------|--------|
| [KPI] | [target] | [actual] |

## Risks and Issues
| Risk or issue | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---------------|--------|------------|-------|
| [description] | [High, Medium, Low, or not stated] | [plan] | [owner or Unassigned] |

## Decisions
- **[Decision]** — Source: [anchor]
  - Reason: [stated reason, or Not stated]
  - Options rejected: [when stated]
  - Dissent: [who disagreed and why, when stated]

## Decisions Needed
- [Decision to escalate] — @[decider]

## Action Items
- [ ] [Task] — @[owner] — Due: [date] — Source: [anchor]

## Clarifying Questions
- [Question]
```
