# Client Call Template

Keep the two sides' commitments apart. A commitment from one side is an action item for that side only.

```markdown
# Client Call: [client]

**Date:** [date] · **Our team:** [names] · **Client team:** [names] · **Status:** Draft

## Objective
[Why we met]

## Client Feedback and Requests
- [Feedback or request]

## Our Commitments
- [ ] [Deliverable] — @[owner] — Due: [date] — Source: [anchor]

## Client Commitments
- [ ] [Deliverable] — @[owner] — Due: [date] — Source: [anchor]

## Open Questions and Risks
- [Question or risk]

## Next Meeting
[Date, time, agenda preview, or No date]

## Clarifying Questions
- [Question]
```
