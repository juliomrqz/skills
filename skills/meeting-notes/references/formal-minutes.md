# Formal Minutes Template

Formal minutes record what the meeting did, not what people said. Leave the discussion out. Write each motion word for word, with its result. Write the vote count when the source gives it, and names per side only for a roll-call vote. The minutes stay a draft until the body approves them.

```markdown
# Minutes: [body name] — [regular or special] meeting

**Date:** [date] · **Place:** [place, or Online] · **Chair:** [name] · **Secretary:** [name]
**Present:** [names] · **Absent:** [names, or Not stated] · **Quorum:** [Present, or Not stated]
**Status:** Draft — not approved

## Previous Minutes
[Approved, Approved as corrected, or Not stated]

## Motions
1. **Motion:** "[exact wording]" — Moved by: [name, or Not stated] — Source: [anchor]
   **Result:** [Adopted | Rejected | Postponed] — Vote: [for–against–abstain, or Not counted]

## Reports Received
- [Report] — Action: [what the meeting did with it]

## Action Items
- [ ] [Task] — @[owner] — Due: [date] — Source: [anchor]

## Adjournment
[Time, or Not stated]

## Clarifying Questions
- [Question]
```
